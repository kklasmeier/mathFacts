    {% call tip("Last sheet must be") %}

    The bar for that last sheet. Good or better is the usual bar. This is checked only after the minimum number of sheets is finished.

    {% endcall %}
    {% call tip("Pair rule") %}

    Any pair uses every combination of the two number ranges. This is the usual choice, and a new stage starts here.

    Sum at most keeps only pairs whose total is at or below the Sum limit. With a limit of 10, 7 + 3 stays and 7 + 5 does not. The standard plans do not use this.

    Doubles makes one fact per first number, with the second number equal to it, such as 4 + 4 or 7 + 7.

    {% endcall %}
    {% call tip("Sum limit") %}

    The largest total kept when Pair rule is Sum at most. Leave this blank for any other pair rule. A blank limit is ignored, and a new stage starts blank. The standard plans leave it blank.

    {% endcall %}
    {% call tip("Review %") %}

    The share of the sheet filled with facts from earlier stages. 20 means about 1 space in 5. On a sheet of 20 problems, about 4 spaces use an earlier stage and the rest come from this stage. A new stage starts at 20. The first stage has no earlier stages, so this number has no effect there.

    {% endcall %}
    {% call tip("Mix Master") %}

    Check this on a stage that reviews several fact sets together. A Good or better sheet here earns the Mix Master badge. The problems stay whatever you set above. Review % is what mixes facts from earlier stages onto the sheet. A new stage leaves this off.

    {% endcall %}
