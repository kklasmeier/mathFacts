Who uses this

Math Facts is a private, self-hosted worksheet tool for one family. A parent or legal guardian creates the account and manages any child profiles.

What is stored

Your email address, username, and a hashed password. Sign-in events, such as the time, network address, and browser type, are kept for security. Child profiles and practice results are added in later phases.

Who can see it

Only people with an account on this server. Nothing is sent to outside services.

Deleting data

An administrator can remove accounts and data. Deletion rules will be added with the administration pages.
